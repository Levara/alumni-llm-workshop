# U.S./Israel-Iran War on Course for Cataclysmic Civilian Harm, Displacement, and Humanitarian Need

**Source:** Refugees International
**Author:** Jeremy Konyndyk, President
**URL:** https://www.refugeesinternational.org/statements-and-news/u-s-israel-iran-war-on-course-for-cataclysmic-civilian-harm-displacement-and-humanitarian-need/
**Date:** March 11, 2026

## Key Findings

Refugees International's president issued a statement condemning civilian harm in the U.S./Israel-Iran conflict, highlighting several major incidents.

### School Strike in Minab, Iran

The organization describes an apparent U.S. strike on a school in Minab that killed approximately 175 people, predominantly children. Konyndyk characterizes this as "the largest number of child casualties in a single U.S. military attack since the My Lai massacre in Vietnam in 1968."

### Broader Pattern of Civilian Targeting

Beyond Minab, the statement documents strikes on "other schools, multiple medical facilities, numerous residential areas, and a water desalination plant." It notes Iranian attacks have similarly targeted civilian infrastructure and residential zones.

### Displacement Crisis

The UN Refugee Agency reports over 884,000 people were displaced within just over a week after fighting began. The statement warns that 1.65 million refugees already in Iran face heightened risks, including 750,000 Afghans with limited rights protections.

## Institutional Concerns

Konyndyk criticizes removal of Pentagon safeguards against civilian harm and notes Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth's disbanding of the office tasked with preventing civilian casualties. The statement also mentions credible reports of reliance on AI targeting models without adequate human oversight.

## Calls for Action

The organization demands:

- Independent investigations into civilian harm incidents
- Congressional hearings on targeting procedures
- Adherence to international humanitarian law protections for civilians
- Restoration of Pentagon civilian protection mechanisms
- Full accountability for strikes on protected civilian infrastructure
