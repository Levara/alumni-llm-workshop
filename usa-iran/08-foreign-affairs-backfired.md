# How America's War on Iran Backfired: Tehran Will Now Set the Terms for Peace

**Source:** Foreign Affairs
**Author:** Nate Swanson
**URL:** https://www.foreignaffairs.com/united-states/how-americas-war-iran-backfired
**Date:** March 17, 2026

## Overview

The article examines America's military confrontation with Iran and its strategic consequences. The author, a former Iran desk officer at the State Department, reflects on how the American military strategy against Iran has backfired, fundamentally altering the balance of power in negotiations.

The author recounts a conversation from roughly seventeen years prior when a veteran colleague advised him: "Stop paying attention to Ahmadinejad. Only focus on Iranian Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei. He makes the important decisions." The colleague noted that Khamenei, then 69, was believed to have serious health issues.

## Main Argument

The central thesis is that the American military strategy against Iran has backfired, fundamentally altering the balance of power in negotiations. Tehran has shifted from a position of defensive constraint to one where it can dictate the terms of any potential peace settlement.

The analysis covers multiple dimensions including:

- U.S. foreign policy strategy failures
- Geopolitical implications for the Middle East
- The role of regional actors like Israel
- Military and strategic miscalculations
- Diplomatic consequences of the use of force
