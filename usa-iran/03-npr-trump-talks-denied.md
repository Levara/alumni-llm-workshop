# Trump Says U.S. in Talks with Iran to End War; Iran Denies It

**Source:** NPR
**URL:** https://www.npr.org/2026/03/23/nx-s1-5757172/iran-defiant-trump-hormuz
**Date:** March 23, 2026

President Trump announced Monday that the United States is negotiating with Iran to resolve the conflict now in its fourth week. He stated the U.S. will delay military strikes on Iranian energy infrastructure for five days while discussions continue.

Trump declared on social media: "VERY GOOD AND PRODUCTIVE CONVERSATIONS REGARDING A COMPLETE AND TOTAL RESOLUTION OF OUR HOSTILITIES IN THE MIDDLE EAST."

He outlined objectives including Iran halting uranium enrichment and the U.S. removing enriched uranium from the country.

## Iran's Response

Iran's Foreign Ministry directly contradicted Trump's claims, stating: "There is no dialogue between Tehran and Washington." Parliamentary speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf characterized reports as "fake news" intended to manipulate markets.

However, NPR confirmed backchannel efforts are underway, with Pakistan, Turkey, and Egypt facilitating behind-the-scenes communications. Egyptian officials indicate efforts toward a potential 30-60 day ceasefire.

## Developments in Lebanon and Beyond

Israel intensified strikes in Lebanon, killing at least 10 people Monday. More than 1,000 people have died and over 1.2 million were displaced across Lebanon.

Iran threatened retaliation against Gulf energy infrastructure if attacked. The International Energy Agency warned the global economy faces a "major, major threat," with oil output losses exceeding combined 1973 and 1979 crises.

Oil prices initially dropped significantly on news of potential negotiations.
