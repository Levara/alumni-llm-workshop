# Maritime Insurers Cancel War Risk Cover in Gulf: Impact on Energy Costs

**Source:** Al Jazeera
**URL:** https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/3/3/maritime-insurers-cancel-war-risk-cover-in-gulf-will-it-spike-energy-cost
**Date:** March 3, 2026

## What's Happening

Insurance firms are withdrawing war risk coverage for vessels in the Gulf following escalating US-Israel-Iran conflict. The Iranian Revolutionary Guard Corps declared the Strait of Hormuz "closed," threatening to set any passing vessel "ablaze."

At least five tankers have sustained damage, two people are dead, and approximately 150 ships remain stranded near the strait.

## The Insurance Response

Marine insurers including Gard, Skuld, NorthStandard, the London P&I Club, and the American Club announced cancellations effective March 5. Ships requiring new coverage will face substantially elevated costs.

War risk premiums have surged to 1% of a vessel's value within 48 hours, up from roughly 0.2% the previous week. For a $100 million tanker, this translates to a voyage premium jumping from approximately $200,000 to $1 million.

David Smith, head of marine brokers McGill and Partners, noted: "each underwriter is invariably increasing rates or...declining to offer terms right now."

## Broader Cost Implications

Marcus Baker, global marine head at Marsh, indicated insurance rates could escalate 50-100% or more. Insurance expenses might rise from 0.25% to 0.5% or even 1% of vessel value.

These increased shipping expenses directly elevate delivered oil and liquefied natural gas costs, subsequently raising fuel, electricity, and heating expenses for consumers globally.

## Strait Significance

The waterway carries approximately one-fifth of global oil consumption plus substantial gas volumes from Gulf producers including Saudi Arabia, Iraq, the UAE, Kuwait, and Qatar.

On Tuesday, President Trump announced the US Development Finance Corporation would provide discounted political risk insurance for maritime commerce in the Gulf, with US Navy escort available if necessary.
