# Lebanon Wracked by Spillover from the Iran War

**Source:** The Soufan Center
**URL:** https://thesoufancenter.org/intelbrief-2026-march-12/
**Date:** March 12, 2026

## Overview

Following Iran's Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei's death in a U.S.-Israeli operation, Hezbollah launched missiles and drones at Israeli territory, triggering widespread conflict that has destabilized Lebanon. The fighting has displaced over 700,000 people and killed approximately 570 Lebanese civilians.

## Military Escalation

Israel responded with intensive airstrikes targeting Hezbollah's stronghold in Beirut's southern suburb of Dahiyeh and launched ground operations in southern Lebanon. Hezbollah retook positions south of the Litani River that it had previously ceded under a November 2024 ceasefire agreement, claiming Israel routinely violated the pact by occupying five high points and conducting frequent strikes.

## Humanitarian Impact

The renewed hostilities have caused significant displacement, with more than 700,000 having fled south Lebanon and Hezbollah strongholds. The Lebanese Disaster Risk Management Unit reported approximately 570 deaths resulting from the violence. Nearly 1 million people -- 20% of Lebanon's entire population -- have been displaced.

## Political Fallout

Lebanese President Joseph Aoun and Prime Minister Nawaf Salam have responded by:

- Outlawing Hezbollah's military and security wings
- Ordering IRGC deportations
- Arresting some Hezbollah members

However, LAF commander General Rodolphe Haykal has refused to deploy troops against Hezbollah while Israeli operations continue, creating a complex internal dynamic.

## Diplomatic Efforts

Lebanon proposed direct talks with Israel for permanent ceasefire and border stability, but U.S. and Israeli officials rejected the overture, preferring to let military operations achieve Hezbollah's disarmament.

## Regional Shifts

Syria's interim government, under Ahmad al-Sharaa, now supports Beirut's efforts to constrain Hezbollah -- a historic reversal from previous conflicts where Syria served as a conduit for Iranian support to the group. This represents a fundamental realignment of regional alliances in the post-Assad era.
