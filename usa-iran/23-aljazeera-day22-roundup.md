# Iran War: What's Happening on Day 22 of US-Israel Attacks

**Source:** Al Jazeera
**URL:** https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/3/21/iran-war-what-is-happening-on-day-22-of-us-israel-attacks
**Date:** March 21, 2026

## Overview

The US-Israel conflict with Iran has entered its fourth week, with over 1,400 reported deaths in Iran. Iran has retaliated with attacks on Israel and US bases, while threatening global shipping and energy infrastructure as millions mark Eid al-Fitr and Nowruz.

## In Iran

- **Casualties**: 1,444 people killed, including at least 204 children
- **UK Base Attack**: Iran fired two ballistic missiles at the Diego Garcia military base in the Indian Ocean
- **70th Wave**: Iranian armed forces announced their 70th wave of attacks, launching missiles and drones toward Israel and Gulf bases
- **Holiday Observances**: Millions gathered at holy sites during the Persian New Year, with many gatherings becoming anti-Western protests

## In the Gulf

- **UAE Threats**: Iran's military warned of "crushing blows" to Ras al-Khaimah if aggression continues from UAE territory
- **Bahrain**: Defense forces destroyed two more missiles; total of 143 missiles and 242 drones destroyed since February 28
- **Saudi Arabia**: Intercepted at least 47 drones, including 38 in three hours
- **Kuwait**: Actively dealing with missile and drone attacks
- **Refinery Strike**: Two waves of Iranian drones hit Kuwait's Mina al-Ahmadi refinery early Friday

## In the United States

- **Trump's Position**: Considering "winding down" operations but ruled out ceasefire; suggested other nations guard the Strait of Hormuz
- **Kharg Island**: Reports indicate US considering blockading or occupying Iran's strategic oil hub
- **Marine Deployment**: US deploying 2,200 to 2,500 additional Marines to the Middle East
- **Ground Troops**: Potential for US boots on the ground to secure the Strait of Hormuz

## In Israel

- **Missile Salvoes**: Nine separate waves of Iranian missiles fired; widespread damage from debris and cluster munitions
- **Shelter Access**: Palestinian citizens demanding equal protection and shelter access

## Oil and Energy Markets

- **Oil Prices**: Brent crude hit $112.19 per barrel
- **Panama Canal**: Operating at capacity with increased LNG tanker demand
- **US Sanctions**: Lifted sanctions on Iranian oil already loaded on tankers
- **Maritime Threats**: 21 confirmed attacks on commercial vessels since March 1
