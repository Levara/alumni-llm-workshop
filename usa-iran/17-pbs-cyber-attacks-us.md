# Iran-Linked Hackers Target U.S. and Allies Amid Ongoing Conflict

**Source:** PBS NewsHour
**URL:** https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/iran-linked-hackers-take-aim-at-u-s-and-other-targets-raising-risk-of-cyberattacks-during-war
**Date:** March 2026

## Overview

Pro-Iranian hacking groups have launched cyberattacks against U.S. targets and Middle Eastern infrastructure since the war began February 28, 2026. The group Handala claimed responsibility for disrupting systems at Stryker, a Michigan medical device manufacturer, stating the attack was retaliation for suspected U.S. strikes killing Iranian schoolchildren.

## Targets and Attack Methods

Beyond Stryker, Iranian-linked hackers have attempted to:

- Penetrate surveillance cameras in Middle Eastern countries to enhance missile targeting capabilities
- Attack data centers across the region
- Target industrial facilities in Israel, educational institutions in Saudi Arabia, and airport infrastructure in Kuwait

These groups openly discuss plans on platforms like Telegram, with one user noting "The datacenters need to be taken out" as they allegedly host "military communication and targeting systems."

## Vulnerable Infrastructure

Cybersecurity experts warn that critical infrastructure remains at significant risk, including:

- Hospitals
- Ports
- Water treatment facilities
- Power stations
- Railways

Local utilities often lack resources for adequate cybersecurity measures, making them attractive targets.

## The Stryker Attack

The Handala hacking group claimed to have exploited Microsoft's cloud management platform, Intune, to remotely wipe more than 200,000 devices across 79 countries. This represents one of the largest single cyberattacks against a U.S. corporation attributed to an Iranian-linked group.

## Expert Assessment

Kevin Mandia, founder of cybersecurity firms Mandiant and Armadin, stated: "Something is going to happen because the gloves are off."

The attack landscape has escalated dramatically since the conflict began, with over 60 Iranian-aligned cyber groups mobilizing within the first days of the war, armed with AI-assisted reconnaissance tools.

## Prevention Recommendations

Security professionals urge organizations to:

- Patch systems promptly
- Maintain current firewalls
- Remove outdated accounts
- Prepare for potential disruptions
- Implement offline backups of critical data
- Monitor for unusual network activity from Middle Eastern IP ranges
