# US-Israel War on Iran: Day 25 of Attacks

**Source:** Al Jazeera
**URL:** https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/3/24/us-israel-war-on-iran-whats-happening-on-day-25-of-attacks
**Date:** March 24, 2026

The US-Israel conflict with Iran entered its 25th day on Tuesday amid conflicting claims about potential peace negotiations.

## Diplomatic Claims and Denials

President Trump claimed discussions were underway with Tehran toward a broader peace agreement, stating "Iran means business." However, Iranian officials firmly rejected these assertions, with the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps and parliamentary leaders dismissing the claims as "fake news" and a "big lie." Iran's position suggests the US is fabricating talks to manipulate oil markets and buy time for troop deployments.

## Military Actions Paused

Trump ordered postponement of planned strikes on Iranian power plants and energy infrastructure for five days, extending an initial 48-hour ultimatum regarding the Strait of Hormuz.

## Iranian Casualties and Damage

The Iranian Red Crescent Society reported that over 82,000 civilian structures have been damaged or destroyed by US-Israeli strikes.

## Regional Impact

### Gulf Nations Under Pressure

Kuwait's air defenses responded to multiple incoming attacks, with alarms sounding at least seven times in one night. Saudi Arabia intercepted approximately 20 drones targeting its Eastern Province. Bahrain has intercepted 153 missiles and 301 drones since conflict onset.

### Economic Consequences

The Strait of Hormuz closure has severely impacted energy-dependent nations:

- South Korea's prime minister cancelled a China trip to address domestic economic fallout
- Japan faces critical supply concerns, with 95% of its oil flowing through the waterway
- The Philippines declared a "national energy emergency"

## Leadership Changes

Iran appointed Mohammad Bagher Zolghadr as the new Supreme National Security Council secretary, replacing Ali Larijani, who was killed March 17.

Pakistan offered to host peace negotiations between the US and Iran for a negotiated settlement.
